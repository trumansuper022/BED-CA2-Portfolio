World Overview

MinePets is a gamified wellness world where good habits power your adventure. Every challenge you create or complete earns Emeralds, and those Emeralds unlock new Minepets, items, and upgrades.

Build your squad by collecting Minepets with different base stats. Use items to boost them, apply upgrades for permanent growth, and store everything in your inventory so your progress is always visible.

When you are ready, take your Minepets into battle. Winning battles grants bonus Emeralds and proves your team is growing stronger with every healthy choice you make.
